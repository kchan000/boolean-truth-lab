Practice settings
+ OR· or adjacent AND' NOT⊕ XOR

Term count controls the major product, sum, or grouped parts in a generated challenge.

Notation and examples

Use A+B for OR, AB or A·B for AND, A' for NOT, and parentheses to group: (A+B)(C'+A). Also accepts ! for NOT, * for AND, and ^ for XOR. Capitals A–D only.

Your workspace

Construct the truth table

Ready when you are. Write an expression for each added column and complete its 0s and 1s.
A valid final-expression column is required. Equivalent expressions count, even when written differently. Checking never fills in your intermediate columns.

How it works

1. The input variables are already filled in, in standard binary-counting order. 2. Add as many expression columns as you need, in whatever order helps you. 3. Enter each expression as a heading and fill every output cell. 4. Check your work to see which cells need attention. The app checks the meaning of each heading and whether your final result is logically equivalent to the challenge.
